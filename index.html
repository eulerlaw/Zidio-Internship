    Questions people ask

    Do I need a team to register?

    Not always. Some events accept individuals, and some let you join as a team leader or team member. The team size is shown on every event.

    What if an event shows “Closed”?

    Registration has ended for it, so the form is replaced with a short message. Keep an eye on the Upcoming events instead.

    Why can’t I submit my form?

    The form checks your name, email, phone number, college and the rules checkbox. The red message under a field tells you what to fix.

    Is this real data?

    No. The events and seat numbers are sample data made for this demo, and registrations are kept only until you refresh the page.
